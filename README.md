# My Favorite Movie

## She's Dating the Gangster

**_She's Dating the Gangster_** is one of my favorite Filipino romantic comedy-drama movies. It tells a story about love, friendship, and unforgettable memories.

### Why I Like It
- Interesting storyline
- Amazing characters
- Romantic and emotional scenes
- Unexpected plot twists

### My Favorite Characters
1. Athena Dizon
2. Kenji Delos Reyes
3. Kelay
4. Kenneth

### My Favorite Quote

> "Sometimes, the people we love leave the biggest memories in our hearts."
